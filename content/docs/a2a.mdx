title: Agent-to-agent (A2A)
description: Connect termsprawl to other machines and agents over the Google A2A protocol — configure peers, send agent output to them, and expose your agents to peers.
---

**Agent-to-agent (A2A)** lets termsprawl talk to agents running elsewhere —
another machine on your network, a teammate's agent, or a service that speaks
the [Google A2A protocol](https://a2a-protocol.org) (JSON-RPC over HTTP).
A2A is how an agent in one termsprawl sends its work to an agent in another.

Two directions are involved:

- **Sending** — your agent forwards its output to a *peer* you configured.
- **Receiving** — peers can see and task your live agents, if you turn that on.

## Configure a peer

1. Open **Settings → Connections → A2A peers**.
2. **Add a peer** with a label, the peer's A2A endpoint URL, and an optional
   bearer token (if the peer gates message sending).
3. Use the **test** button next to a peer to discover the peer's agent card
   and confirm the connection works.
4. **Remove** a peer when you no longer need it.

<Callout type="info" title="Peer tokens stay secret">
  A peer token is stored in your settings, never logged. For an extra layer,
  an environment variable per peer wins over the stored token:
  `TERMSPRAWL_A2A_PEER_TOKEN_<ID>` (the peer id uppercased, non-alphanumerics
  as underscores).
</Callout>

## Send an agent's output to a peer

1. Right-click an **agent node** on the canvas.
2. Choose **A2A send to peer ▸**.
3. Pick the peer from the submenu.

The agent's latest output is forwarded to the peer, and the context menu shows
the result (`✓` / `✗` with a short summary). If you have not added any peers
yet, the submenu says so and points you at Settings.

A2A is also available as a **node link** behavior: connect an agent terminal to
a compatible node with a link and choose *Send to A2A peer* to forward content
automatically or on demand — see [Node links](/docs/node-links).

## Receive tasks from peers

Exposing your agents is **off by default**. When you enable it, termsprawl
lists your live agent terminals at a **localhost-only** A2A endpoint, and
peers on the machine can send tasks to them over the Google A2A protocol.

To turn it on: open **Settings → General → Expose agent nodes to A2A peers**.
The endpoint's access token is written to `a2a-agent.json` in termsprawl's
user-data folder.

<Callout type="warn" title="Receiving is an opt-in local endpoint">
  Nothing listens until you flip the toggle, and even then the endpoint binds
  to localhost only — a peer elsewhere on your network cannot reach it
  directly. Exposing to remote machines is a deliberate network-setup
  decision beyond the app's default.
</Callout>
