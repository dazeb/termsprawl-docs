Termsprawl Cloud is an optional account layer that backs up your workspace so
you can restore it elsewhere. This page explains what's backed up, how
encryption works, and how to enable it.

## Sign in

Sign in with GitHub — it's a device flow, so no password is typed into
termsprawl.

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Find the Cloud section and click **sign in**.
3. termsprawl shows a device code and opens GitHub for you to approve it.
4. Once approved, you're signed in. The panel shows your GitHub login and plan.

## What's backed up

The cloud backup covers your workspace state:

- The list of projects (projects, folders, and remote SSH projects).
- Each project's canvas layout.
- App settings and preferences.

<Callout type="info" title="What isn't backed up">
  The cloud backs up your workspace state and layouts — not your files or your
  shell history. Your code and documents stay on your machine.
</Callout>

## Encryption

Backups are encrypted server-side with your key. Your workspace data leaves
your machine only in encrypted form, and the service can't read it without your
key. This is the "settings/layout/project backup" phase — the data that's
backed up is the state that makes termsprawl yours, not your source code.

## Back up now

Push a fresh snapshot of your workspace to the cloud on demand with **Back up
now** in Settings. There's no scheduled automatic backup yet — run it when you
want a checkpoint.

## Plans

Cloud accounts have a **Free** tier and a **Pro** tier (Pro adds more backup
storage and longer retention). The status line in Settings shows which plan
you're on.

<Callout type="tip" title="Why this matters">
  Closed apps lose nothing because your sessions live in tmux. The cloud layer
  is the next step: move to a new machine, sign in, and your whole workspace —
  layouts, projects, settings — comes back with you.
</Callout>
