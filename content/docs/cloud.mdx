Termsprawl Cloud is an optional account layer: encrypted workspace backup, and
— on the Canvas plan — your own hosted canvas space that runs in a browser.
This page explains what's backed up, how encryption works, how spaces work,
and how to enable it all.

## Sign in

Sign in with GitHub — it's a device flow, so no password is typed into
termsprawl.

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Find the Cloud section and click **sign in**.
3. termsprawl shows a device code and opens GitHub for you to approve it.
4. Once approved, you're signed in. The panel shows your GitHub login and plan.

## What's backed up

The cloud backup covers your workspace state:

- The list of projects (projects, folders, and remote SSH projects).
- Each project's canvas layout.
- App settings and preferences.

<Callout type="info" title="What isn't backed up">
  The cloud backs up your workspace state and layouts — not your files or your
  shell history. Your code and documents stay on your machine.
</Callout>

## Encryption

Backups are encrypted server-side with your key. Your workspace data leaves
your machine only in encrypted form, and the service can't read it without your
key. This is the "settings/layout/project backup" phase — the data that's
backed up is the state that makes termsprawl yours, not your source code.

## Back up now

Push a fresh snapshot of your workspace to the cloud on demand with **Back up
now** in Settings. There's no scheduled automatic backup yet — run it when you
want a checkpoint.

## Plans

Cloud accounts have three tiers:

- **Free** — sign in, and that's it.
- **Pro** — encrypted cloud backup + sync for your workspace ($2/mo).
- **Canvas** — everything in Pro **plus your own online canvas space** ($5/mo).

The status line in Settings shows which plan you're on, and upgrading is a
checkout away from the same panel.

## Online canvas spaces

The **Canvas** plan gives you a hosted termsprawl canvas that runs in your
browser — same nodes, same terminals, reachable from any machine.

1. In Settings, under the cloud section, choose **open your online canvas**.
   termsprawl provisions a private space for you (first time takes under a
   minute).
2. Your canvas opens at `canvas.termsprawl.com/<your-login>` in the browser.
3. Work there like the desktop app — terminals, sticky notes, agents, and
   projects all work, and the layout is saved as you go.

### Keeping desktop and online canvas in sync

Settings shows the space rows for the signed-in user:

- **Open your online canvas** — provisions (or reopens) the space and signs
  you in with a short-lived token.
- **Open online snapshot** — pulls the space's latest canvas into a NEW local
  project, so you can look at (or fork) the online state without touching
  your current work.
- **Sync this project online** — pushes the active project's canvas to your
  space.

<Callout type="info" title="Snapshot sync, not live merge">
  Sync is pull-model snapshots: the online canvas stores the latest snapshot
  of your workspace, and the desktop pushes or pulls whole snapshots. Your
  terminals' tmux sessions stay on the machine they run on — the online
  canvas runs its own terminals in the hosted space.
</Callout>

### Spaces lifecycle

- **Stop** — the space pauses (no resource use); your canvas is kept, and
  reopening it brings everything back. The dashboard's SpaceCard has a
  confirm-gated Stop button.
- **Idle spaces stop themselves** — a space nobody touches for 30 minutes is
  stopped automatically; opening it again wakes it (takes a few seconds) with
  your canvas intact.
- **Deleting your account** stops the space and removes its data after a
  7-day grace window.

<Callout type="tip" title="Why this matters">
  Closed apps lose nothing because your sessions live in tmux. The cloud layer
  is the next step: move to a new machine, sign in, and your whole workspace —
  layouts, projects, settings — comes back with you.
</Callout>
